# Member Quick Start

Your Discord server's administrators decide which features and commands are available. If a command is missing, ask them to check its Discord Integration permissions.

## Register your character

Use `/register`, select your Albion Online server, and enter your Albion Online character name. If several matches are returned, select the correct character from the results.

Registration links a character to your Discord user in this Discord server. It does not automatically grant every membership or feature. Your administrators must configure the relevant Albion Online guild, Albion Online alliance, or custom member group.

If your server uses applications, start with its application button and follow the questions instead. A reviewer decides the application. If a character needs recovery from an earlier registration or departure, ask an officer; self-registration cannot recover every state.

## Check your profile

- `/membership` shows your registered characters, memberships, positions, and account information.
- `/roles` shows your membership roles and reaction-role subscriptions.
- `/balance` shows your character-account balances.
- `/statement` provides account history for an eligible character.

You can register up to 25 characters in this Discord server. The first is initially your main character; officers can change your main character and nickname settings. Your profile and balances are specific to this Discord server.

## Use the feature panels

Look for the panels your administrators have configured:

| Panel or message | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Content | Host scheduled or unscheduled parties; join another party from its roles message |
| Accounts | View balances and statements, or give funds from an eligible owned character |
| Re-gears | Select eligible content and a character, then submit the required evidence |
| Weapon Specialisation | Submit proof or inspect your recorded specialisations |
| Giveaways | Host when eligible; enter an announcement by reacting with 🎁 when you have active managed membership |
| Application or ticket button | Open the configured private conversation |

Opening a panel does not grant eligibility. A feature can require registration, active membership, character ownership, or another permission. Follow the relevant [guide]({{ '/guides/' | relative_url }}) for its rules.

## Join content

Open the party thread and use **Join**, **Standby**, or **Leave** on its roles message. If the host requires approval, your request stays pending until the host accepts it; it does not reserve a place.

Scheduled dates and time inputs use UTC. Discord's displayed timestamps appear in your local time zone. See [Parties and Templates]({{ '/guides/parties-templates/' | relative_url }}).

## Get help

For registration, missing roles, recovery, or denied actions, contact your Discord server's officers. Provide the command or button you used and the result, without sharing tokens or database credentials.
